1. Introduction

Bingo 25 respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Bingo 25, how that information is used, and the choices available to you. Bingo 25 is an independently developed game and service, and this policy describes the data handling practices associated with the game and its online features.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly when using the game, information generated through gameplay, and technical information required to operate, secure, and improve the service. This may include account-related information, gameplay data, profile information, friend and room data, and diagnostics needed to maintain the app and its online features.

3. Account Information

When you create or access a Bingo 25 account, we may process information associated with that account, such as login identifiers, authentication details, profile references, and account-related metadata required to provide access and maintain account security. Where applicable, authentication providers may be used to support sign-in, account creation, and related service functionality.

4. Profile Information

We may store profile information such as usernames, player identifiers, XP, trophies, achievements, leaderboard status, and other in-game profile details that help personalize the experience and support progression in Bingo 25.

5. Friends and Social Features

To provide friend requests, friends lists, invites, private rooms, and social interactions, we may process information related to your social connections, room membership, invitations, and interaction history as required for these features to function.

6. Online Multiplayer Data

Multiplayer functionality requires match and session information such as match identifiers, player participation, room information, lobby status, gameplay events, and game state information. This data is required to support realtime multiplayer gameplay, match progression, and online interaction between players.

7. Game Progression and Leaderboards

Bingo 25 may process XP, achievements, trophies, scores, rankings, and leaderboard information to support progression, competition, and game functionality. This information may be used to calculate player rank, unlock progression milestones, and maintain fair gameplay.

8. Device and Diagnostic Information

We may collect technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, language settings, crash reports, performance diagnostics, and related technical data when necessary to maintain and improve the service. We do not claim to collect specific data unless it is actually necessary for the functionality and reliability of the app.

We may use information to provide and operate Bingo 25, support authentication, enable multiplayer functionality, maintain social features, save player progression, maintain leaderboards, prevent abuse and cheating, troubleshoot technical issues, improve service reliability, and respond to support requests.

12. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only where necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve the game and related services. We do not sell personal information.
